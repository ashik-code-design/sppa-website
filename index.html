Features

Everything a staff member needs

Mark a full batch, search past records, and export a clean PDF — all in one place.

⚡

Live data

Departments, staff, labs and experiments load straight from the backend.

🔢

Auto register numbers

Roll numbers get the correct department prefix automatically.

✅

Built-in validation

Preparation and Output are capped and combined into Total before saving.

📝

Mark entry & details

Enter marks per experiment, then search and edit records later.

📄

PDF export

Download a print-ready mark sheet for any class.

🔁

Score conversion

Rescale any Total to a different maximum instantly.

How it works

From class selection to a finished PDF

1

Select the class

Choose department, section, staff, lab and experiment.

2

Mark the batch

Score each student's Preparation and Output out of 5.

3

Total automatically

Preparation + Output combine into Total, capped at 10.

4

Search the records

Pull up any class's marks in Mark Details by experiment or all at once.

5

Export & share

Download a print-ready PDF for records or moderation.
